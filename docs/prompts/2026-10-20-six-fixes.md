## 2. Decision log

Rows 1-11: no questions were put to the user; each is a choice made against the first brief.
Rows 12-16 are the second round, asked with mock-ups of the real tab bar.

| # | Question | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Why did back close the app from an open transaction? | Reproduced on the phone: after Android recreated the screen, the old back listener was still subscribed and answered first. Back now goes only to the newest listener |
| 2 | What does back do with nothing behind? | Ele says "Press back again to close EleFi."; a second press within 2.5 seconds closes. Moving anywhere cancels it |
| 3 | What does "the page before" mean? | The pages in the order they were opened, up to 30. Closing a transaction or a capture steps back rather than opening the list again; Plans tabs and guide steps replace in place so back does not walk through them |
| 4 | What is read again on return from recents? | Every list and figure page. Capture, Edit, Quick capture and other forms are never read again, so nothing half typed is lost |
| 5 | Which colour for Quick? | Amber, a disc behind the bolt, and amber when it is open too, so it is never the purple of the tab that is open. The open tab also has a bar above it |
| 6 | Three or five suggestions? | Three, the same as Paid to |
| 7 | Priority for Note, Bought through, Paid with | This contact's history first, then the most used across all transactions. A new or half-typed contact gets the most used straight away. A Self Transfer has no contact, so it gets the most used |
| 8 | Quick capture's kinds | A switch under the title: Debit (first), Credit, Self Transfer. Debit asks Paid from and Paid to; Credit asks Received into and Received from; Self Transfer asks Source and Destination, both containers |
| 9 | Filters: dropdowns how? | One row per filter saying what is chosen ("Any" when nothing), dropping its options open underneath, one open at a time, rather than a second sheet. Dates closes after a choice; the others stay open for more |
| 10 | Where does Clear filters go? | In the sheet's head, beside the cross; greyed out when nothing is filtered |
| 11 | Needs review in the filters | A switch row, "Needs review only", not an option inside Kind |
| 12 | Quick's colour: Q1 soft sand, Q2 dusty teal, Q3 muted rose, Q4 sage green? | **Q2 dusty teal**, a faded disc. Replaces row 5's amber |
| 13 † | The open tab as a path: P1 road narrowing in, P2 with a centre line, P3 soft filled road, P4 curves rising? | None ⤵ |
| 14 | The open tab, simpler: N1 colour only, N2 soft pill behind the icon, N3 small dot? | **N2**, and the pill glides from tab to tab as the page opens; Quick is the exception ⤵ |
| 15 | The Drive page before it is built: shown greyed, or settings that work with a button that does not? | **Shown, greyed**, under "Coming soon" |
| 16 | Where do Export and Restore go? | Into Backup and Restore, under Google Drive backup. Your data keeps labels, contacts and deleted |
| 17 | Ele's sound in Settings ⤵ | A round speaker button that mutes and unmutes (speaker with waves, speaker with a cross), not a third switch; one line under it: "A short sound each time Ele pops up." |
